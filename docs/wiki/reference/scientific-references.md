# Method references

Module pages contain method-specific references and interpretation cautions.
Core primary sources include:

- GWAS-VCF: [Lyon et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-020-02248-0)
- LD blocks: [Berisa and Pickrell 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btv546)
- LDSC: [Bulik-Sullivan et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3211)
- SuSiE: [Wang et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1111/rssb.12388)
- FINEMAP: [Benner et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btw018)
- MAGMA: [de Leeuw et al. 2015](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1004219)
- FUMA MAGMA cell typing: [Watanabe et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11181-1)
- scDRS: [Zhang et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01167-z) and the
  PostGWAS [scDRS implementation evidence review](scdrs-evidence-review.md)
- GCTA fastBAT: [Bakshi et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep32894)
- mBAT-combo: [Li et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2022.12.006)
- PoPS: [Weeks et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01443-6)
- MiXeR: [Frei et al. 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10310-0)
- GSA-MiXeR: [Nature Genetics 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01771-1)

External software manuals and database websites can change. Record the tool
version and documentation release used for each analysis. Do not treat a Wiki
summary as a substitute for the method paper's assumptions or the installed
tool's exact manual.
